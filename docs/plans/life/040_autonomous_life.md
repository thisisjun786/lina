## Authored autonomous rules

Add `WorldPackV3 = Omit<WorldPackV2,"schemaVersion"> & {schemaVersion:3; autonomy:AutonomyDefinition}`. V1/V2 bytes and behavior remain readable. V3 retains the identical supported social grammar; compiler/version checks accept both social-capable versions without silently upgrading stored packs. A V2 world remains available for manual social resolution, and reports that autonomous rules are unconfigured.

`AutonomyDefinition` version 1 has:

- `needs`: authored numeric axes with `id`, `label`, finite `min/max/initial` and `driftPerStep`; per-agent initial rows come from the active cast. No built-in hunger, romance, economy or fatigue rules.
- `goals`: initial `id`, `agentId`, description, nonnegative priority and referenced family IDs. Progress is bounded 0–1; statuses are active/completed/abandoned. New personal goals from accepted model proposals have owned experience evidence and known family references.
- `events`: one policy per family, explicit cooldown in accepted steps, novelty penalty, goal weight and lists of need/trait/habit weight terms. Terms reference real axes/habits and use finite coefficients. A listed capability set constrains actor proposals for this opportunity; it is not an authoritative resolved-action menu.
- `quietWeight`: explicit nonnegative weight for choosing quiet time even when events are eligible.
- `growth`: explicit numeric maximum change per step and minimum distinct experience count for habit change. Existing identity locks and axis bounds remain authoritative.

The v3 suggestion prompt explains these fields and unresolved choices. Confirmation/preview checks all references, nonnegative weights, finite totals and bounded Cartesian expansions before inserting a pack. Confirmed edits preserve old meanings and accepted state: adding needs/goals initializes only new rows; retiring an actor preserves its history but excludes it from new choices. Existing need meanings/bounds/initial/drift and goal identities cannot be reinterpreted by an update. Event weights/cooldowns/growth policies may change only at a confirmed pack boundary, invalidating pending steps. Old immutable packs remain available to replay.

Do not send global candidate lists, hidden values, world/step/checkpoint digests or target private state. Do not silently change a requested primitive to a canned action. At most the configured number of actor actions/primitives and causal depth are admitted. Target response uses a fresh target-only observation plus the observable attempt; it cannot include the initiator's private rationale, goals or secret claim contents before a validated reveal. Explicit rejection remains separate from private authoritative rule failure.

Use the 030 social service for staged resolution. Then collect a separately scoped reflection from each actual participant who receives an observation, when its call is configured and reserved. Model proposals can create own inferred claims of unknown truth, beliefs about already known claims, goals/need changes and trait/habit growth. Social mapped attitude rules still use 030 validation; reflection cannot independently overwrite those axes or desynchronize the social record. A changed habit/trait must appear in the next actor request and alter a controlled decision in tests. A proposed growth record without sufficient evidence is rejected, not stored as established personality.

Family/rule effects may assign typed variables, create allowed simulated facts, update validated attitudes/goals and queue follow-up events. Preserve evaluator visibility rules and independently validate each applied effect against its authored source. Reflection text cannot pretend to be an authored effect. Rule-induced attitude changes on an Ensemble-mapped axis must use the supported social resolution path, or reject the incompatible authored rule at activation; do not let two effect paths own the same mapped value.
